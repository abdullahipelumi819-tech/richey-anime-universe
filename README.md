# Richey Anime Universe

Welcome to the **Richey Anime Universe**, an original fantasy world built around powerful characters, unique cultivation systems, forgotten histories, and stories that expand beyond a single world.

## About the Universe

Richey Anime Universe is an independent original anime-inspired fantasy project featuring multiple stories, characters, worlds, and power systems.

Each story has its own identity, characters, conflicts, and path to power.

The universe is designed to grow over time through storytelling, artwork, character development, and future visual projects.

## Featured Stories

### The World That Forgot Richey

**Protagonist:** Richey Adeyemi  
**Power System:** Echo Path  
**World:** Elyndra

Richey Adeyemi is a boy whose family was destroyed because of a forbidden bloodline feared by King Pelumi.

Carrying a mysterious pendant given to him by his mother, Richey begins a journey to uncover the truth about his family, his forgotten past, and the power hidden within him.

His journey begins with one question:

**Why did the world forget Richey?**

### Chaos Lord

**Protagonist:** Kael Veyron  
**Power System:** Chaos Path

In a world where chaos is more than destruction, one young man begins a journey toward a power that few are capable of understanding.

The path ahead will challenge his strength, his identity, and his place in a world ruled by powerful forces.
