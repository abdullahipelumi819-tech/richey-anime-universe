THE WORLD THAT FORGOT RICHEY

Chapter 1: The Boy the World Forgot

Before Richey could discover who he was, someone decided he should never exist.

They say some memories are meant to be forgotten. But Richey's past was never truly gone.

Years ago, the Adeyemi family carried a forbidden bloodline — a bloodline connected to ancient forces known as Echoes.

Their power was feared by rulers who believed that if the bloodline ever awakened, the balance of the world could change forever.

Richey was still young when his mother, Mira, realized that danger was coming.

She placed a mysterious bead in his hand and told him to keep it close.

"When everything seems lost, this will remember you." — Mira Adeyemi

Then King Pelumi's forces arrived.

The attack destroyed the Adeyemi Realm.

Richey's father fought to protect his family while Mira tried to get her son to safety.

In the final moments, Richey's father used his power to hide him from the attackers.

Richey survived.

But he lost everything he knew.

The Ruins

When Richey finally awakens, he finds himself among the ruins of his former home.

There is no one left to answer his questions. His parents are gone. His home has been destroyed. Only the mysterious bead remains.

Richey searches through the ashes, desperately hoping to find a sign that his family survived.

"Mom?" Richey

Silence.

Then the bead begins to glow.

A strange vision appears before Richey's eyes.

He sees a fragment of the past.

His mother's Echo appears.

And she looks directly at him.

"If you're seeing this... they failed to erase you." — Mira's Echo

Veyra

Before Richey can understand what he has seen, something catches his attention among the ashes.

A mysterious weapon lies buried beneath the ruins.

Richey reaches toward it.

The weapon is called Veyra.

The moment his hand touches the weapon, the ground begins to tremble.

The air changes.

For a moment, it feels as if the world itself has recognized him.

Richey does not know who is trying to erase him.

He does not know why King Pelumi fears his family.

And he does not know what his mother's mysterious bead truly contains.

But he knows one thing.

"I will find the truth." — Richey Adeyemi

His journey has begun.
