A Story Waiting to Be Remembered

Richey Adeyemi is a young survivor whose life changes forever when his family becomes the target of a powerful force determined to erase the Adeyemi bloodline.

Richey survives the destruction of his home, but the answers he searches for may be more dangerous than the enemies hunting him.
