THE UNIVERSE

Welcome to Elyndra

Elyndra is a world where memories can leave behind powerful traces called Echoes. Those who can interact with these Echoes can uncover pieces of the past — or change what the world remembers.

Richey's journey will introduce new characters, mysterious powers, dangerous enemies and secrets that will slowly reveal the truth behind his existence.

This is only the beginning.
