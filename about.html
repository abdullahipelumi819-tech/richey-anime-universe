About Richey Anime Universe

Richey Anime Universe is an original story project built around anime-inspired worlds, emotional characters, mystery, action and unusual cultivation systems.

Our goal

Build a library of ten memorable stories, grow a real reader community, and eventually expand the strongest stories into bigger formats such as videos and animation.

Original work

The stories and characters on this website are original project material. As the universe grows, more chapters, characters, illustrations and story worlds will be added.
