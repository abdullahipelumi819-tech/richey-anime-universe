The World That Forgot Richey

A boy who lost everything begins a journey to discover the truth behind his forgotten past.

CHAPTER 1

The Boy the World Forgot

Power System: Echo Path

MAIN CHARACTERS

Characters

Richey Adeyemi

The survivor carrying Veyra and his mother's mysterious pendant.

Smartolino

Richey's mysterious companion with an extraordinary calculating mind.

CHAPTER 1

The Boy the World Forgot

They say some memories are meant to be forgotten. But Richey's past was never truly gone.

Richey stands among the ruins of a burning kingdom.

Years Earlier

Years ago, the Adeyemi family possessed a forbidden bloodline. A power so dangerous that even kings feared its awakening.

Richey's mother, Mira, places a mysterious pendant in his hand.

Mira: “Richey, keep this with you. One day, when everything seems lost, it may save your life.”

Young Richey: “Why are you giving this to me, Mom?”

Mira: “Because some things are better understood when you're older.”

The King

King Pelumi sits upon his throne.

Pelumi: “The Adeyemi bloodline must disappear before its power awakens.”

Soldier: “And the boy?”

Pelumi: “Leave no survivor.”

The Adeyemi realm had no idea that an army was already marching toward its gates.

The War

That night, the war began.

Mira: “Take Richey! Now!”

Richey's Father: “I will protect him.”

The Fall

Father: “Forgive me, my son.”

Richey is sealed away.

His father sacrificed everything to hide him from the king.

Richey Awakens

The seal disappears. Richey wakes up.

Richey: “Mom?”

Silence.

Richey: “Dad?”

He sees the burning ruins.

Richey: “Where are you?!”

The Promise

Richey discovers the symbol of King Pelumi's kingdom.

Richey: “Pelumi...”

Richey: “You took everything from me.”

The pendant begins glowing.

But Richey didn't know that his mother's gift was hiding something far greater than protection.

Richey: “What... is happening to me?”

“I don't know where you are...”

“I don't know why this happened...”

“But I swear...”

“I will find the truth.”

That day, a boy who had lost everything began a journey that would eventually shake the entire universe.

His name was Richey Adeyemi.

CHAPTER 2

The First Hunter

After leaving the ruins of his home, Richey spent days searching for answers.

Smartolino: “You're Richey Adeyemi, aren't you?”

Richey: “Who told you my name?”

Smartolino: “Nobody. I figured it out.”

Richey: “That's not an answer.”

Smartolino: “You'll learn that I rarely give simple answers.”

Smartolino suddenly becomes serious.

Smartolino: “We're being followed.”

A shadow moves through the forest.

Opidex: “Richey Adeyemi.”

Richey: “How do you know my name?”

Opidex: “Because your existence has been causing problems for a very long time.”

Opidex: “Give me the weapon.”

Richey: “Come and take it.”

Richey draws Veyra.

CLANG!

Opidex touches Veyra.

CRACK!

Richey: “What did you do?!”

Opidex: “I found its weakness.”

Smartolino: “Thirty-two possible attacks.”

Richey: “Which one works?”

Smartolino: “None.”

Richey: “Then why are you standing there?!”

Smartolino: “Because I'm looking for the one that hasn't happened yet.”

Smartolino: “He doesn't want to kill you.”

Richey: “Then what does he want?”

Smartolino: “Your weapon.”

Smartolino: “Throw Veyra.”

Richey: “Are you crazy?”

Smartolino: “Trust me.”

Richey throws Veyra. Opidex immediately goes for it.

Smartolino: “Now!”

Richey touches an ancient Echo.

For the first time, Richey didn't simply see an Echo.

He touched it.

An ancient structure appears.

Opidex: “Impossible...”

The Echo collapses.

Smartolino: “RUN!”

Opidex: “So you really can touch the Forgotten.”

Opidex: “King Pelumi was right.”

Veyra City

Richey and Smartolino finally reached the gates of Veyra City.

Richey stopped.

Something about the city felt strangely familiar.

He looked at the enormous walls surrounding it.

Richey: “Have I been here before?”

Smartolino looked at him.

Smartolino: “That's the question, isn't it?”

They walked through the gates.

People moved through the streets, but something felt different.

Several people stopped and stared at Richey.

Not because they recognized his face.

Their eyes were fixed on the sword at his side.

A warrior stepped forward.

Warrior: “That sword...”

He stared at Veyra.

Warrior: “Veyra.”

Richey looked down at his sword.

Richey: “You know it?”

The warrior's expression changed.

Warrior: “That sword belonged to our King.”

The street suddenly became silent.

More people gathered.

An old man slowly walked through the crowd.

His eyes were fixed on Veyra.

Old Man: “After all these years...”

Richey: “You're mistaken. I'm not your king.”

The old man looked at Richey's face.

Old Man: “Perhaps your face has changed.”

He looked at Veyra again.

Old Man: “Perhaps your memories are gone.”

The old man stepped closer.

Old Man: “But Veyra cannot be mistaken.”

Richey remained silent.

Old Man: “That sword belonged to King Richey.”

A sudden pain struck Richey's head.

For a moment, he saw a throne.

An army kneeling.

A battlefield covered in smoke.

A younger version of himself standing with Veyra in his hand.

Then the vision disappeared.

Richey grabbed his head.

Richey: “What was that?”

The old man stared at him.

Old Man: “You truly don't remember.”

The Destroyer

Before anyone could say another word—

BOOM!

The city gates exploded.

Fire spread across the entrance.

People screamed and ran.

A dark figure stepped through the flames.

Graven.

Graven: “Richey.”

Richey turned.

Graven smiled.

Graven: “Come and die.”

The old man stepped forward.

Old Man: “You will not touch him.”

Graven looked at him.

Graven: “Move.”

Old Man: “No.”

Graven raised one hand.

A wave of flames exploded across the street.

The old man blocked the attack.

BOOM!

Buildings shook.

Graven: “You're protecting this loser?”

Old Man: “He is the reason we are alive.”

Graven laughed.

Graven: “Is it worth wasting your life over him?”

Old Man: “Yes.”

Graven's smile disappeared.

Graven: “Then die with the rest.”

Another wave of flames erupted.

The old man rushed forward.

The two collided.

BOOM!

Their battle shook Veyra City.

Richey watched in disbelief.

Richey: “Why is he fighting for me?”

Richey looked around.

Smartolino was nowhere to be seen.

The old man suddenly shouted.

Old Man: “Take the sword and go!”

Richey: “I'm not leaving you!”

Old Man: “You don't understand what is coming!”

The old man pointed toward an ancient palace hidden deeper inside the city.

Old Man: “Go there!”

The Ancient Tomb

Richey ran toward the ancient palace.

Inside, everything was covered in dust.

Ancient weapons lined the walls.

Strange symbols covered the floor.

At the end of the palace stood a massive stone door.

Richey touched it.

The door slowly opened.

Behind it was an ancient tomb.

A strange pressure filled the room.

Richey walked deeper.

Then he saw it.

A massive seal carved into the ground.

Something was trapped beneath it.

Richey: “What is this?”

The seal reacted to Veyra.

The sword began to tremble.

Richey stepped closer.

A faint voice echoed from beneath the tomb.

“Not yet...”

Richey froze.

Richey: “Who are you?”

Silence.

The seal began to crack.

Only slightly.

Richey stepped back.

He didn't understand what he was seeing.

The Last Stand

Outside, the battle continued.

Graven's flames spread through Veyra City.

The old man struggled to stand.

Graven walked toward him.

Graven: “You're finished.”

The old man raised his weapon again.

Old Man: “Not yet.”

Graven flicked his hand.

BOOM!

The old man's body was destroyed.

He turned to ashes.

Rage

Richey heard the explosion from inside the tomb.

His eyes widened.

Richey: “No...”

He ran out of the tomb.

He saw the ashes falling through the air.

Richey: “YOU!”

Graven turned.

Richey charged.

Veyra flashed in his hand.

CLANG!

Graven blocked the strike.

He smiled.

Graven: “Interesting.”

Richey attacked again.

And again.

Graven avoided every attack.

Then—

BOOM!

Richey was thrown through a wall.

He crashed onto the ground.

Blood covered his face.

Graven walked toward him.

Graven: “Is that all?”

Richey slowly stood.

He looked at the ashes of the old man.

Richey: “I won't let you kill anyone else.”

Richey charged again.

Graven struck him.

Richey fell.

Blood dripped from his mouth.

A drop landed on Veyra.

The Awakening

For a moment—

Nothing happened.

Then the sky changed.

KRA-KOOOOOM!

Lightning struck Veyra.

Richey froze.

The seal beneath the ancient tomb shattered.

Something awakened.

A powerful presence entered the sword.

Veyra began to glow.

Richey: “What... is happening?”

Energy rushed through his body.

The air around him began to distort.

The Forgotten appeared throughout Veyra City.

One by one—

They were pulled toward Richey.

Richey began absorbing their Echoes.

His body began to change.

The ground cracked beneath his feet.

Graven's expression changed for the first time.

Graven: “Stop.”

Richey couldn't hear him.

More Echoes rushed toward him.

The energy continued growing.

Graven clenched his fist.

Graven: “I can't let this continue.”

He turned toward the battlefield.

Graven: “Bida!”

A massive figure appeared behind him.

Graven: “Kill him.”

The Spear

Bida charged.

The attack came straight toward Richey.

Then—

CLANG!

A spear blocked the attack.

Smartolino stood between Richey and Bida.

Smartolino: “If you want to kill him...”

He tightened his grip on his Echo spear.

Smartolino: “...you have to go through me first.”

Graven smiled.

Graven: “Then die.”

Smartolino raised his spear.

Behind him, Richey's transformation continued.

Veyra pulsed with ancient power.

The Echoes continued disappearing into Richey's body.

And somewhere deep beneath Veyra City...

something that had been sleeping for centuries...

opened its eyes.

Chapter 3 — The Awakening

Smartolino stood between Graven and Richey.

The ruins of Veyra City burned around them.

Smoke covered the sky.

The streets were filled with broken stones, destroyed buildings, and the ashes of those who had fallen.

Behind Smartolino, Richey remained motionless.

His body was surrounded by a strange energy.

The sword Veyra floated beside him.

Every few seconds, the sword released a pulse of ancient power.

Graven stared at Richey.

His expression changed.

He could feel it.

Richey's consciousness was returning.

Graven tightened his grip around Bida, his sword.

“I don't have time for this.”

Smartolino raised his Echo spear.

“If you want to kill him…”

He stepped forward.

“You'll have to go through me first.”

Graven looked at him for a moment.

Then he raised Bida.

“Then die.”

Graven disappeared.

BOOM!

Smartolino barely blocked the first strike.

The force of the attack sent him flying across the street.

He crashed through the remains of a building and landed heavily on the ground.

Before he could recover—

Graven appeared above him.

Bida came down like a falling blade.

CLANG!

Smartolino blocked with his spear.

The ground beneath him cracked.

Graven pushed harder.

“You're wasting my time.”

Smartolino gritted his teeth.

“I won't let you touch him.”

Graven's eyes became cold.

“Then I'll remove you first.”

He swung Bida again.

Smartolino dodged.

The blade destroyed the ground where his head had been.

Smartolino counterattacked.

His spear struck toward Graven's chest.

Graven moved slightly.

The spear missed.

Graven kicked Smartolino in the stomach.

BOOM!

Smartolino was launched through several walls.

He landed on his back.

Blood ran from his mouth.

He slowly stood.

His hands were shaking.

But he still raised his spear.

Graven looked toward Richey.

The energy around Richey had become stronger.

The Echoes surrounding the battlefield were being pulled toward him.

Graven's expression became serious.

“He's waking up.”

Smartolino heard him.

Graven looked at him.

“If that boy regains consciousness…”

He lifted Bida.

“…this entire battle changes.”

Smartolino smiled faintly.

“Then you better hurry.”

Graven's eyes narrowed.

“I intend to.”

He vanished again.

Smartolino raised his spear.

CLANG!

Bida struck.

Smartolino blocked.

Another strike.

He blocked again.

Another.

His spear began to crack.

CRACK.

Smartolino looked at it.

A deep fracture had appeared across the weapon.

Graven didn't stop.

He attacked faster.

Smartolino was forced backward.

Every strike became heavier.

Every movement became harder to follow.

Finally—

BANG!

Graven's blade smashed Smartolino into the ground.

A crater formed beneath him.

Smartolino struggled to move.

Graven stood over him.

“This is your last chance.”

Smartolino slowly looked toward Richey.

Richey's eyes were still closed.

But his fingers had begun moving.

Smartolino noticed.

He smiled.

“No.”

Graven followed his gaze.

Richey's hand slowly moved toward Veyra.

Graven's expression darkened.

“Enough.”

He raised Bida.

His killing intent filled the entire battlefield.

“I'll kill you now.”

Smartolino forced himself onto one knee.

He knew he couldn't win.

He knew his spear was almost broken.

But he also knew one thing.

If he moved—

Graven would reach Richey.

So Smartolino stood again.

Graven stared at him.

“Why?”

Smartolino wiped the blood from his mouth.

“Because he's not awake yet.”

Graven's eyes became colder.

“Exactly.”

He disappeared.

Bida came toward Smartolino's neck.

But—

CLANG!

Smartolino blocked the blade at the last possible moment.

The force pushed him down to one knee.

Graven pushed Bida harder.

“Die!”

Smartolino's arms trembled.

His spear was about to break.

Then—

THUMP.

Everything stopped.

Graven felt it.

Smartolino felt it.

Even the flames seemed to become silent.

Behind them—

Richey's heart had beaten once.

THUMP.

A second pulse followed.

THUMP.

The Echoes around the battlefield suddenly froze.

Graven slowly turned his head.

Richey's eyes were still closed.

But Veyra had begun floating upward.

A strange light surrounded the sword.

Graven's grip tightened.

“No…”

Richey's body suddenly lifted from the ground.

The air around him twisted.

The ground cracked beneath his feet.

Smartolino stared.

“What is happening?”

Graven stepped away from him.

His attention was completely focused on Richey.

The energy surrounding Richey became stronger.

One Echo disappeared into his body.

Then another.

Then another.

Hundreds of Echoes began moving toward him.

Richey screamed.

“AAAGHH!”

A massive shockwave exploded from his body.

Graven crossed Bida in front of himself.

Smartolino was thrown backward.

The entire battlefield shook.

Richey's body remained suspended in the air.

Then Veyra suddenly flew toward him.

Richey reached out.

The moment his hand touched the sword—

The world disappeared.

Richey stood inside a vast throne room.

Thousands of people were kneeling before him.

A crown rested upon a throne.

An army stood behind it.

Richey looked around.

“Where am I?”

No answer.

He stepped forward.

Then he saw someone sitting on the throne.

The person's face was hidden in darkness.

Richey stared.

“Who are you?”

The figure slowly raised its head.

A voice echoed through the room.

“Remember.”

Richey froze.

The throne room began collapsing.

The army disappeared.

The crown shattered.

And just before everything vanished—

Richey heard one final word.

“King.”

Richey's eyes suddenly opened.

Back in Veyra City—

Graven felt the change.

His expression became completely serious.

Richey was awake.

But something was different.

Richey slowly looked down at Veyra.

He didn't understand why—

but the sword felt familiar.

Almost like he had held it a thousand times before.

Graven raised Bida.

“Now I really have to kill you.”

Richey looked at him.

For a moment, neither of them moved.

Then Richey tightened his grip around Veyra.

And beneath Veyra City—

something ancient opened its eyes.
