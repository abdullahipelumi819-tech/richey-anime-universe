ABOUT THE UNIVERSE

Richey Anime Universe

An original universe of fantasy, mystery, action, powerful characters and stories waiting to be discovered.

THE VISION

A Universe Built From Original Stories

Richey Anime Universe is an original story project created to bring together different worlds, characters, powers and unforgettable adventures.

Each story has its own identity, characters and special power system. The goal is to create a universe where every story feels different while still belonging to something bigger.

01

Original Worlds

Every story introduces its own world, characters, conflicts and mysteries instead of simply repeating the same formula.

02

Unique Power Systems

The stories use different cultivation and power systems, giving each world its own way of becoming stronger.

03

Characters With Purpose

Heroes, villains and companions are created with their own goals, secrets, struggles and reasons for the choices they make.

04

A Growing Universe

The universe will continue to grow with new chapters, new characters, new stories and eventually larger creative projects.

THE FUTURE

More Than Just Stories

Richey Anime Universe is being developed as a long-term creative project. The stories begin here, but the vision can eventually expand into illustrations, videos, animation and other forms of storytelling.
