REN EGADES REALM · CHAPTER 1

The World That Forgot Richey

The destruction of the Adeyemi Realm is only the beginning.

They say some memories are meant to be forgotten. But Richey's past was never truly gone.

Years ago, the Adeyemi family guarded a forbidden bloodline. Their power was connected to something far older than kings and kingdoms: the Echoes left behind by everything the universe had tried to forget.

Richey was still a child when strange things began happening around him. People sometimes forgot meeting him. Drawings of him seemed to change. His mother, Mira, became increasingly worried.

“When everything seems lost, this will remember you.” — Mira Adeyemi

Then King Pelumi's forces arrived.

The attack destroyed the Adeyemi Realm. Richey's father, Kael, used his Resonance to hide his son's existence, while Mira gave Richey a mysterious bead called The Last Memory.

Years later, Richey awakens among the ruins of his former home. His parents are gone. His kingdom is gone. But the bead remains.

When Richey touches a broken object buried in the ruins, his first Resonance awakens: Echo Trace. He sees a fragment of the past — but his mother's Echo looks directly at him.

“If you're seeing this... they failed to erase you.” — Mira's Echo

A mysterious black blade appears in the ashes. Its name is Veyra. When Richey touches its handle, the world around him reacts as though reality itself has recognized him.

Richey doesn't know who is trying to erase him, why Pelumi fears his existence, or whether his parents are truly gone.

His journey has begun.
