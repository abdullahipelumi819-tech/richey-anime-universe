OUR WORLD

Welcome to Elyndra

Elyndra is a world where reality carries memories called Echoes. Powerful people known as Resonants can connect with these Echoes, but every use of Resonance has a cost.

Follow Richey as he searches for the truth behind the destruction of his family — and discovers why the universe is trying to erase his existence.
